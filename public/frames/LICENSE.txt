Laptop frame
Front-view render of “MacBook Air M2” by rtql8d.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/macbook-air-m2-786fa23d402a4f90ae36c4168997f9cc
https://sketchfab.com/rtql8d
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The file used is the adaptation published at https://www.tomasod.com/models/laptop/macbook-air-m2.glb
(aluminum roughness 0.5, keyboard roughness 0.75). The screen glass was cleared so a screenshot can sit in the opening.
It is not an official Apple asset and is not endorsed by Apple Inc.

Apple Design Resources were not used. Their license allows mockups of interfaces for Apple operating systems only, and it does not allow embedding the bezels in a website.
